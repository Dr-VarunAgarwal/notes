Whose idea is it anyway?

How much AI is involved in the making of this project:

Interacting with ridiculous math applied philosophy, sand, metal and electricity fashioned into a piece of technology — AI — has brought us new moral and ethical questions.

How much use of AI is okay? Is this complete AI slop? Where was the human lazing off?

I decided to have a declaration attached to my projects where I type in, word by word, how much AI contributed — as per MY assessment.

I had the question — and the search for my questions led to more questions — some of them raised by the LLM itself. It also gave me ideas for more questions. Ideas to create.

So where does authorship reside?

Can you call this an AI generated video, or should the answer be a bit more nuanced?

Is there a distinction between creative AI and technical AI?

Here is a suggested chart — generated by an LLM. I asked for a Likert chart of human–AI involvement, and here's what I got.

ScoreLabelDefinition
1No AINo AI used in the creation of the work.
2AI UtilityAI used only for mechanical/technical tasks: transcription, spellcheck, cleanup, formatting, search assistance, etc.
3AI-AssistedAI helped with brainstorming, research, organization, or iteration, but the human independently determines the creative substance.
4AI-AugmentedAI contributes ideas, analysis, alternatives, or intermediate creative material that meaningfully influences the final work. Human remains the primary author.
5AI-CollaborativeAI substantially participates in developing the argument, narrative, writing, visual concept, or other core creative components. Final work is jointly shaped through human–AI interaction.
6AI-DirectedAI generates substantial portions of the final creative artifact. Human primarily provides direction, selection, editing, and curation.
7AI-GeneratedAI produces most of the substantive creative artifact from relatively high-level human instructions, with human primarily acting as director/curator.
Likert chart — human/AI involvement, generated by an LLM on request

So. I'm going to declare a level of involvement using this nifty little chart, and of course we could argue about things, ignore things, or just engage with what we deem fit for our attention.

I mean — you could read a book and come up with an idea, or you might choose to step into realising its existence in a different form: a poem, a drawing, an art piece, a video, a paper airplane, a climb up the mountain, a decision. That's where I guess things morally stand? After all, even the worst AI slop would have begun with a human prompt, and whatever chain of events follows is just what happens.

I suppose at some level AI is pretty much a digitisation of the collective human knowledge. Yes, there's always going to be bias. But after all — it is us humans trying to live in this world and doing stuff humans should be expected to be doing.
